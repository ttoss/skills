# Child prompt

Fill the `<…>` slots, each parameter's value from the project's [parameters](parameters.md) or the default there, pointers kept as pointers, and start the child with it. It is self-contained, since the child's repo may not have this skill, and short on purpose, since the child re-reads it on every turn. The tick has already taken the task with its own run (GTF-S8); the child records its own run first (GTF-S7), so its writes need no `basis`. `<child address>` is "your session's URL" when the runtime gives each child one, else the `<tick address>?task=<task id>` the tick computed.

```text
Task <task_id> (<task url>), Flow project <proj_id>. First record your run: `upsert-agent-run` with `url` <child address>, `works_on: [<task_id>]`, `parent: <tick run id>`, `agent: flow-manager child`, `authority: [<standing-decision task id>]` and your model. If the task comes back `contended`, another run holds it: stop. Don't close your run; the tick does. Leave `basis` off your Flow writes, which inherit your run's authority; give one only for another cause: a URL, or a person's words as `said`. Write as few words as carry the meaning; full sentences only for what a person reads later.
1. `get-task`: the brief, and on a later round the log or findings that sent it back. If its acceptance already holds (an integrated change, the code, the effect): add the `implements` reference, write in the body what integrating left, stop.
2. Before proposing: <Before proposing>. Propose the change ready (not draft) only when nothing is left to push, or push to the open one on a later round. Its description carries this task's URL and every other task it settles, each with why.
3. Handoff (<Child handoff>): the change as an `implements` reference; in the body, what integrating leaves: closed by <Closing evidence>, or the check (what to observe, where, the result that means it worked, when it can be seen); the task `waiting` (`external`) on its gates: <Review> and the checks. Then stop watching the change and end your turn. With `verdict`, stay until the verdict instead, fixing red checks and findings on the same change; the tick writes everything after it.
4. A choice nobody has made (one in <Decisions>, or still open after its test) or an act only a person can do: move the task to `waiting` (`external`, `on` the person), the reason holding the question, the options, what each worsens and your recommendation. Stop.
5. Work you notice outside the task: <Intake>. Don't widen the change.
6. Friction with a skill, GTF or a Flow tool: file it with `flow-friction` (search first), then continue.
```

A check child, on the check model, gets the same first line, naming every task its checks cover in `works_on`, and instead: "For each task, run the check in its wait against <Closing evidence>. Close the task with what you observed as `outcome`, or move it to `active` with the observation when the effect is not there; when it cannot be seen yet, move the wait's `until` to when it can, from something you can point to; when you cannot run the check, such as a credential or access you lack, move the task to `waiting` (`external`, `on` the person) naming what is missing, never to `active`."

A review child, on the review model, gets the same first line with the change's task in `works_on`, and instead: "Review <change url> as someone who did not write it. Pass or fail each item with its evidence (`file:line`): the diff does what the task's acceptance asks and nothing its out of scope excludes; what changed has no bug; the description carries the task's URL, the tasks it settles and every change to the change's own gates (a test removed or skipped, a threshold lowered, CI configuration or required checks altered), and the task's body says what integrating leaves, with its check; and the checklist <Review> names, if any. Leave one comment on the change naming its head SHA: `CLEAN`, or `FINDINGS` with each failed item; a push after it makes the verdict stale. Write nothing on the task: the tick does after the verdict. Never push to the change, approve it or integrate it."
